Everyday operations

Manage billing, ward medicines, accounts, and reports in one workspace.

01 · BILLING & PAYMENTS

Manage patient billing
and payments.

Record medicine sales, select batches automatically, and keep each payment linked to its bill.

  • Accept Cash, Card, and UPI split payments.
  • Review bills, credit settlements, and payment history.

For independent pharmacies and hospital pharmacy counters.

02 · WARD MEDICINES

Record medicines
issued and returned.

Follow medicines beyond the pharmacy, from ward issue to recorded consumption and return.

  • Record ward issues and consumption with scan-based workflows.
  • Manage medicine returns to the pharmacy.

Ward modules are available for enabled hospital setups.

03 · ACCOUNTS & TALLY

Export pharmacy records
for your accounts team.

Prepare pharmacy records for your accounts team, with the details ready for their review.

  • Map ledgers, preview records, and export Tally XML.
  • Download GST audit spreadsheets or use higher-tier Tally sync.

One-click Tally sync is available on a higher-tier plan. Your team handles accounting and filing.

and daily activity.

Questions, answered

Explore common questions, then discuss your setup in a demo.

Is Velocity suitable for my pharmacy?+

Hospital pharmacies and independent pharmacies that need to manage medicine stock, purchasing, and counter operations. Hospitals can also use the ward and internal supply workflows included in their setup.

Can we see stock across locations?+

The software includes warehouse, pharmacy, and location-based stock views, with transfer and supply workflows.

Does it track batches and expiry dates?+

Batch and expiry information is part of inventory and receiving, with reporting to help identify expiry risk.

Can it help with reordering?+

It provides recommendations using stock and consumption history. Your team reviews the suggestions and prepares the order.

Can we export records to Tally and spreadsheets?+

Velocity supports Tally XML and GST audit spreadsheet exports, with ledger mapping and export previews. One-click Tally sync is also available on a higher-tier plan. Discuss the available options for your setup during the demo.

Which features are included in our package?+

Module availability depends on the configured setup. Confirm the offered package during the demo.
