ASTR AI DESIGN GUIDELINE

1. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY: "ANCIENT PRECISION MEETS FUTURE LOGIC"
The ASTR AI brand identity bridges the gap between classical engineering and futuristic deep-tech. The UI should feel like a Technical Audit Journal or a Holographic Command Interface, combining organic serif typography with rigid, monospaced technical metadata.

2. COLOR PALETTE

PRIMARY NEUTRALS (THE VOID)
- Main Background: #050505
- Rich Black: #1a1a1a
- Card Background: #181818

BRAND ACCENTS (THE ASTRA GOLD)
- Primary Gold: #E6CD99
- Dim Gold: #cba864
- Bright Gold: #fff2d1
- Gold Glow: rgba(230, 205, 153, 0.35)
- Gold BG: rgba(230, 205, 153, 0.06)

SECONDARY ACCENTS
- Muted White: #D1D5DB
- System Green: rgba(76, 175, 80, 0.4)
- System Red: #EF4444

3. TYPOGRAPHY

HEADING STYLES
- Primary Display: Cinzel (Serif)
- Technical Headers: Space Grotesk (Sans-Serif)

BODY & CONTENT
- Main Body: Lora (Serif)
- Metadata / Labels: JetBrains Mono (Monospace)
- Style: Uppercase, tracking [2px - 6px]

ANNOTATIONS
- Handwritten Accents: Caveat (Cursive)
- Style: -rotate-2 to -rotate-6, opacity-40 to opacity-60

4. UI COMPONENTS & LAYOUT

GLASSMORPHISM (LIQUID GLASS)
- Style: backdrop-blur-[24px], bg-white/5, border-white/10
- Interaction: On hover, borders shift toward Gold and backgrounds brighten

TECHNICAL ELEMENTS
- Blueprint Grids: 1px patterns at 3% opacity
- Metadata Bars: Headers with Reference IDs and Status indicators

BUTTONS
- Shape: Full pill (rounded-full)
- Animation: Light-streak skew on hover

5. VISUAL LANGUAGE & IMAGERY
- Audit Style: 21:9 aspect ratio images
- Overlays: 2% grain, 10% scanlines, and ethereal gold glows

6. MOTION & INTERACTION
- Reveals: Staggered GSAP translateY(40px)
- Viscous Highlights: "Liquid Droplet" transitions for navigation
- Ambient: Drifting stars and nebula orbs (20s-100s)
- Micro-interactions: Subtle -translate-y-1 and shadow expansion
