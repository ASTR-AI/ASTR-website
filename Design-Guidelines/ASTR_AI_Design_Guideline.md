  # ASTR AI Design Guideline

  ## 1. Design Philosophy: "Ancient Precision meets Future Logic"
  The ASTR AI brand identity bridges the gap between classical engineering (precision, mission-criticality) and futuristic deep-tech. The UI should feel like a **Technical Audit Journal** or a **Holographic Command Interface**, combining organic serif typography with rigid, monospaced technical metadata.

  ## 2. Color Palette

  ### Primary Neutrals (The Void)
  - **Main Background:** #050505 (Deepest Black)
  - **Rich Black:** #1a1a1a (Panel Backgrounds)
  - **Card Background:** #181818 (Component Surfaces)

  ### Brand Accents (The Astra Gold)
  - **Primary Gold:** #E6CD99 (Primary call-to-actions, highlights)
  - **Dim Gold:** #cba864 (Borders, secondary details)
  - **Bright Gold:** #fff2d1 (Interactive glows, high-energy states)
  - **Gold Glow:** rgba(230, 205, 153, 0.35)
  - **Gold BG:** rgba(230, 205, 153, 0.06)

  ### Secondary Accents
  - **Muted White:** #D1D5DB (Body text)
  - **System Green:** rgba(76, 175, 80, 0.4) (Status: Nominal / Success)
  - **System Red:** #EF4444 (Error / Critical / Connection Failure)

  ## 3. Typography

  ### Heading Styles
  - **Primary Display:** Cinzel (Serif)
    - Usage: Large hero titles, Section headers.
    - Feeling: Authoritative, Ancient, Precise.
  - **Technical Headers:** Space Grotesk (Sans-Serif)
    - Usage: Solution titles, modern UI components.

  ### Body & Content
  - **Main Body:** Lora (Serif)
    - Usage: General reading, paragraphs, long-form content.
  - **Metadata / Labels:** JetBrains Mono (Monospace)
    - Usage: Audit logs, technical references, system status.
    - Style: Uppercase, tracking [2px - 6px].

  ### Annotations
  - **Handwritten Accents:** Caveat (Cursive)
    - Usage: Marginal notes, "Architect's Notes."
    - Style: -rotate-2 to -rotate-6, opacity-40 to opacity-60.

  ## 4. UI Components & Layout

  ### Glassmorphism (Liquid Glass)
  - **Core Style:** backdrop-blur-[24px], bg-white/5, border-white/10.
  - **Shadows:** 0_8px_32px_rgba(0,0,0,0.4) with inset highlights.
  - **Interaction:** On hover, borders shift toward Gold and backgrounds brighten.

  ### Technical Elements
  - **Blueprint Grids:** Radial or linear patterns (1px dots every 40-60px) at 3% opacity.
  - **Metadata Bars:** Section headers containing Reference IDs and Status indicators.

  ### Buttons (Liquid-Glass CTA)
  - **Shape:** Full pill (rounded-full).
  - **Style:** Minimalist borders with viscous hover effects.
  - **Animation:** Light-streak skew passing through on hover.

  ## 5. Visual Language & Imagery
  - **Audit Style:** 21:9 wide aspect ratio images.
  - **Schematics:** Isometric wireframes or SVG blueprints for mockups.
  - **Overlays:** Subtle grain (2%), scanlines (10%), and ethereal gold glows.

  ## 6. Motion & Interaction
  - **Reveals:** Staggered GSAP translateY(40px) animations.
  - **Viscous Highlights:** "Liquid Droplet" transitions for navigation.
  - **Ambient:** Slow drifting stars and nebula orbs (20s-100s).
  - **Micro-interactions:** Subtle -translate-y-1 and shadow expansion on hover.
